Snip the Sprout: Gardening the Subconscious

If you plant a tomato seed, you do not get a rose. Elsie Lincoln Benedict builds a whole practice on that plain fact in How to Get Anything You Want, and it turns out to be the most hands-on idea in the book. She calls the subconscious a garden, governed by the Law of the Seed: every seed brings forth fruit of its own nature. Every feeling you plant is a seed. And a seed does not negotiate — it grows into the exact thing it was, no matter how much you wish it had become something nicer. Plant resentment and you do not harvest peace.
