How I think about AI

Three voices I keep close.

Individual AI wins don't add up to institutional intelligence on their own. These three keep me honest about the gap, and remind me what to do next.

  1. 1

    Purpose before tools

    Exponential Organizations

    The ExO model starts with a Massive Transformative Purpose. AI sits inside the model as one lever, Algorithms, alongside staff on demand, community, and experimentation. Name the purpose first, then pick the algorithm.

    Write the purpose down before you open a new tool.

    Read about ExO
  2. 2

    Data first, then prompts

    Christopher S. Penn, Trust Insights

    Chris, co-founder and chief data scientist at Trust Insights, treats AI as a data problem before a tools problem. His RAPPEL framework (Role, Action, Prime, Prompt, Evaluate, Learn) turns a one-off chat into a repeatable process, and the 5P lens keeps the work tied to a purpose and a measure.

    Prime the model with my own data, then turn what worked into a process.

    Trust Insights
  3. 3

    Learn it by doing it

    Ruben Hassid, How to AI

    Ruben's advice is practical: test the tools yourself, follow exact click-by-click steps, and copy workflows that already work. That is how one person becomes the colleague everyone asks about AI.

    Turn one working workflow into a reusable skill every week.

    How to AI
